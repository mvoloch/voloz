# Fonts

Variable WOFF2 files (Latin and Latin Extended subsets) from Fontsource
(`@fontsource-variable/*`), bundled so the app works offline. All three are
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org

- Bricolage Grotesque, by Mathieu Triay (display headings in Voloz Lite)
- Onest, by Dmitri Voloshin (text in Voloz Lite)
- JetBrains Mono, by JetBrains (keys, times, model names in Voloz Lite)
